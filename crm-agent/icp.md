# Target companies

- size:
  - Primary: 10-250 employees.
  - Secondary: 250-1,000 employees (executive retreats).
  - Teams under ~10 are not a priority unless well-funded.
- stage:
  - Seed (selectively), Series A, Series B, Series C, growth-stage, and select later-stage/private companies with innovation-focused cultures.
- industry or vertical:
  - B2B SaaS, AI/ML, developer tools, cybersecurity, fintech, healthtech, climatetech, enterprise software, infrastructure/cloud, venture capital firms, startup accelerators.
- geographies:
  - Primary: New York City metro.
  - Secondary: Boston, Philadelphia, Washington D.C., Toronto.
  - Executive retreats: San Francisco/Bay Area.
  - Other major North American tech hubs.
- exclusion criteria (companies the agent should never alert on):
  - Traditional manufacturing, retail chains, restaurants, hospitality operators, government agencies, K-12 schools, local service businesses.
  - Companies with little collaborative knowledge work (i.e. no distributed/office-based leadership team doing strategic planning).
  - Companies too small to realistically book retreats or memberships (see size floor above).
  - Businesses outside North America, unless specifically targeted.

# Target roles

- decision-maker titles (used for high-risk classification):
  - Founder, Co-Founder, CEO, President, COO, Chief of Staff, Head of Operations, VP Operations, Head of People, Chief People Officer, VP People, Head of Workplace, Director of Workplace Experience, Executive Assistant to CEO/Founder (often owns logistics and vendor selection).
- champion titles (used for champion alerts):
  - People Operations Manager, HR Business Partner, Office Manager, Workplace Experience Manager, Employee Experience Manager, Executive Business Partner, Team Operations Manager, Program Manager, Culture & Community Manager.
- user titles:
  - Employees, leadership team members, advisors, board members, retreat attendees, guests, company event organizers.
- exclusion roles (e.g. "anyone in legal at existing customers"):
  - Legal Counsel, General Counsel, Compliance, Procurement (unless already engaged in a deal), Finance/AP contacts, IT support, recruiting coordinators, customer support, vendors/consultants unrelated to purchasing.
  - Existing member contacts who are only operational users, unless they become an expansion opportunity.

# What counts as a champion move worth alerting on

- A past champion (role_category=champion in contacts.csv) joins a company that matches the ICP above.
- A past champion gets promoted into a champion or decision-maker title inside a company that's already in the pipeline.
- A past attendee (someone who has personally experienced an Atwoods retreat, whether or not they were the original buyer) shows up as a decision-maker or champion title at a new company matching the ICP — this is a warmer signal than a cold contact who has never attended, since they can vouch for the experience firsthand.
